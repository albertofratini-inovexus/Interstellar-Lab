Startup Profile

Interstellar Lab — Company Overview

Extracted from the intro call between Barbara Belvisi and Philippe Roche (Sep 28, 2026), the Investment Brief (Seed-3 extension) and interstellarlab.com. Deck figures are company claims; cost per kg and contract values are still pending from the founder.

Sector & Sub-sector

Controlled-environment agriculture / AgTech hardware + SpaceTech: AI-controlled closed-loop greenhouses (BioPods) for rare botanicals on Earth and plant-growth payloads in orbit and on the Moon

Stage & Round

Late seed / Series Seed-3 extension (Preferred, 1x) at $45M pre / $81.4M post; ~$2.9M open until 30 Nov 2026; $14.3M + €450K raised to date; $30–40M Series A targeted for 2027

Business Model

Hardware + recurring service: SIRIUS research pod ($150K + $50K/yr), ORION production pod ($500K + $150K/yr); per-mission space payloads (EDEN from $500K; BLOOM lunar, mission-based); grant/prize funding (NUCLEUS)

Geographic Focus

Delaware C-Corp with French opco (Ivry-sur-Seine design & manufacturing); NASA Kennedy Space Center lab; small NYC office opening; Grasse production sites

Problem & Value Proposition

Fragile, climate-exposed botanical supply chains for cosmetics, fragrance and nutraceutical groups; BioPods claim x14–x54 yields per m², up to 99% less water, no pesticides, traceable local production

Product & Technology

Closed-loop climate, water and nutrient control with CO2 capture; plant-recipe database, AI models, swarm optimisation across the pod fleet, computer vision; modular Earth-to-orbit hardware

Target Customer

Earth: L'Oréal (Lancôme), Robertet, DSM-Firmenich, then New Jersey fragrance houses and nutraceutical players. Space: NASA, Vast (Haven-1), Astrolab (FLEX rover), Axiom, Starlab/Voyager

Traction

FY2025 French-entity revenue €1.55M (+76%), ~€1M at the US entity per founder; ~€2.0M burn; $2M 'already sold' for 2026; NASA Deep Space Food Challenge Phase 3 US winner

Team

Barbara Belvisi (solo founder & CEO, ex-hardware VC: Hardware Club/HCVC, Hello Tomorrow, TheFamily); Jim Rhoné (CPO, ex-Soliquid); ~25–28 staff; board: Bruno Maisonnier, Anousheh Ansari, Greg Autry

Explicit Bottlenecks

(1) Production delays and pilot-to-park scale-up; (2) unproven cost per kg vs field/biotech; (3) concentration on 1–2 lumpy contracts; (4) no CFO / C-level software lead; (5) space revenue tied to partner timelines; (6) US terrestrial GTM not started

Methodology

How Mentors Were Scored

  • Coverage: all 537 Affinity mentor records, joined to all 3,338 note rows and the 542 enriched profiles. Inovexus staff were excluded (2 records), leaving 535 mentors scored, 500 of them with notes.
  • Layer 1 (notes, primary): notes were cut at administrative markers for theme scoring; full untruncated notes were used for the space re-sweep. Nine theme lexicons (space, agri/CEA, cosmetics/fragrance/ingredients, biotech, hardware/industrialisation, finance, US, climate, enterprise B2B) built a 208-profile candidate pool, and every profile in that pool was read individually. The other 327 were swept for industrial, CFO, US and life-science signals, which added 10 more.
  • Space re-sweep (v2): all 537 records were re-scanned for 40+ space terms across every CSV column, every enriched-profile field and the full untruncated notes. This added Thomas Nathan (Airbus/NASA), Rui Duarte (microgravity-medicine stake), Marie Gepel (Thales data & AI) and Patrick Renault (aerospace working-capital finance). 'SpaceTech' ticked only as a self-declared Typeform interest (e.g. Fabio Lalli, Ivan Communod, Steve Vaile, Serge Borg, Peter Block) was not credited without supporting evidence.
  • Layer 2 (CSV, secondary): Manual industry ×0.45, Typeform industry ×0.15, manual skills ×0.25, Typeform skills ×0.10, geography ×0.03 and business model ×0.02 were used to validate, never to override, notes evidence.
  • Contamination control: a portfolio meeting about a maritime/satellite-imaging startup is attached to several mentors' notes (e.g. Gaurang Telang, Mark Dunning, Jacques-Alexandre Gerber). Those satellite mentions were not credited. The same applies to internal Inovexus meetings that mention Series A, L'Oréal or the US.
  • Granola: a broad multi-sector query (space, CEA/agtech, fragrance & ingredients, plant biotech, hardware scale-up, CFO/deeptech fundraising) surfaced the Granola-only pipeline above.
  • Anti-recycling: the shortlist was built from Interstellar's specific bottlenecks. Generic B2B SaaS sales profiles that recur in recent reports were left out unless they had sector-specific evidence.
